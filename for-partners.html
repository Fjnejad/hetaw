For partners

Validated with centres. Paid for by the system that saves the years.

HETAW is validated first on retrospective data from certified endometriosis centres, and built for integration into selective-care contracts with statutory health insurers.

Who we work with

Certified endometriosis centres

Retrospective validation on your cohorts, clinical review of the question set, joint publication. In return: better-prepared referrals with a documented symptom history.

Health insurers (Krankenkassen)

Every year of diagnostic delay is a year of paid but ineffective care. The intended route is a validation cooperation under §68a SGB V leading to a selective-care pilot under §140a SGB V, with outcomes measurable against claims data.

Research partners

Epidemiologists, general-practice and gynaecology research groups: the question set, the validation design and the results are developed as open research.

Complementing existing pathways

Programmes such as ENDO-EVE standardise the initial assessment in gynaecology practices; HETAW adds the GP entry point and, later, integration of work-up results.
