Who we work with

Four kinds of partner, one validation plan

Certified endometriosis centres

Retrospective validation on your cohorts, clinical review of the question set, joint publication. In return: better-prepared referrals with a documented symptom history.

Krankenkassen

Every year of diagnostic delay is a year of paid but ineffective care. The route: a validation cooperation under §68a SGB V leading to a selective-care pilot under §140a SGB V, measurable against claims data.

Research partners

Epidemiologists, general-practice and gynaecology research groups: question set, validation design and results are developed as open research.

Existing pathways

Programmes such as ENDO-EVE standardise the initial assessment in gynaecology practices; HETAW adds the GP entry point and, later, integration of work-up results.

Research & validation

Validated before it is used

The symptom question set is built from published evidence and clinical review, then evaluated on retrospective data from certified endometriosis centres before any practice uses it. Results are published openly, with a model card.

The method in detail
  1. Now

    First-visit symptom triage

    Question set development and retrospective validation with endometriosis centres.

  2. Next

    After the work-up

    Combining routine labs, ultrasound and MRI findings into a post-test likelihood.

  3. Later

    Care-pathway integration

    Pilot within a selective-care contract; CE marking as a medical device (Class IIa planned).

What a partnership looks like

Clear roles, no data leaving your institution

Your data stays with you

Retrospective analyses run under your governance — on site or on pseudonymised extracts under a data-use agreement. HETAW receives results, not records.

Pre-registered protocol

Endpoints, cohort definition and analysis plan are fixed and registered before the data is touched; the publication is joint.

Light on your time

A first call, a feasibility check on the cohort, then a defined review of the question set by one or two clinicians — a handful of hours in total.
