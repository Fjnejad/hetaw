About

Founded in Berlin by a data scientist who read the diagnostic-delay numbers and did not accept them.

Founder

HETAW is founded by Fahrnaz Jayrannejad, a Berlin-based data scientist with an MSc in AI and a background in software engineering, proteomics and bioinformatics, after eight years building machine-learning systems in industry. She works on HETAW full time.

The company works with clinicians, epidemiologists and endometriosis centres in Berlin and across Germany, and is building its scientific advisory board.

The name

Health · Earlier · Through · Awareness

HETAW stands for what the product is meant to do: bring health earlier, by making a pattern visible that today goes unseen. The eclipse in the logo is the disease hidden behind everyday complaints; the line that follows is the moment it is recognised.
