About

Founded in Berlin by a data scientist who read the diagnostic-delay numbers and did not accept them.

HETAW is a pre-incorporation digital-health venture in Berlin. It is built as research first, with clinicians, epidemiologists and endometriosis centres.

Founder

Fahrnaz Jayrannejad

Berlin-based data scientist with an MSc in AI and a background in software engineering, proteomics and bioinformatics, after eight years building machine-learning systems in industry. She works on HETAW full time.

The company works with clinicians, epidemiologists and endometriosis centres in Berlin and across Germany, and is building its scientific advisory board.

The name

Health · Earlier · Through · Awareness

HETAW™ stands for what the product is meant to do: bring health earlier, by making a pattern visible that today goes unseen. The eclipse in the logo is the disease hidden behind everyday complaints; the line that follows is the moment it is recognised.

Where we are

Research stage, Berlin, November 2026

Question set in development

Built from published evidence; clinical review and retrospective validation with endometriosis centres are the next steps.

Regulatory path defined

Planned as a medical device under the MDR (Class IIa) and as a high-risk AI system under the EU AI Act; interpretability and a model card are design requirements.

Name protected

HETAW is filed as an EU trade mark (EUIPO No. 019447062). The company is in formation; the site is run by the founder personally until then.
