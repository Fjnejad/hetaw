For practices

A structured reason to consider endometriosis — in the time of a normal consultation.

GPs and office-based gynaecologists are not endometriosis specialists and have few minutes per patient. HETAW gives them a consistent question set and a score they can document and act on.

Who it helps

The two doors most women walk through first

General practitioners

Often the first contact for pain, fatigue and cycle complaints — and the entry point where endometriosis is least likely to be considered. HETAW gives the GP a structured reason to refer earlier.

Gynaecology practices

A consistent, documented first-visit assessment that fits the consultation and makes the case for work-up or referral explicit — including for patients with regular cycles.

In practice

What it asks of you, and what it gives back

Two to three minutes

A defined set of questions on pain pattern, cycle, digestive, urinary and fatigue symptoms, reproductive history and prior consultations, asked during the consultation.

No integration project

For the pilot HETAW runs as a standalone application; the doctor enters the history directly. Integration with practice-management systems through the standard German interfaces follows once the question set is validated.

A documented decision

A risk score with the reasons behind it, in plain language — a defensible basis for watching, investigating or referring, and a lower risk of both missing a case and over-referring.
