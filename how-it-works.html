How it works

Three minutes at the first visit. One interpretable score.

HETAW is a decision-support app for GPs and gynaecologists. It does not diagnose and does not use insurer data: everything it scores is entered by the doctor in the consultation.

The problem

Endometriosis presents as scattered complaints across several specialties. Each visit sees one piece.

Endometriosis is tissue similar to the uterine lining growing outside the uterus — on the ovaries, the bowel, the bladder, the peritoneum, sometimes the diaphragm or nerves. Because the lesions are spread through the body, so are the symptoms. A woman sees one doctor per complaint, and nobody connects the fragments.

The first consultation is where the pattern is most often missed, and where it could most easily be caught.

The method

A structured history in, a score with reasons out.

i.

The doctor enters a short symptom history

A defined set of questions on pain pattern, cycle, digestive, urinary and fatigue symptoms, reproductive history and prior consultations — designed to be asked in two to three minutes and to capture the fragments that today go unconnected.

ii.

HETAW returns a risk score with its reasons

A calibrated pre-test risk score and the symptom patterns that drove it, in plain language: which answers raised the score, which lowered it, and how confident the estimate is.

iii.

The doctor decides

Watch and re-evaluate, order ultrasound or blood work, or refer to a certified endometriosis centre. HETAW supports the decision; it never makes it, and it does not diagnose.

Three design principles

The doctor stays in charge.

HETAW does not diagnose and does not communicate with the patient. It gives the doctor a structured, defensible basis for a decision he or she makes anyway.

Everything scored is entered by the doctor.

No insurer, claims or electronic-record data. Consent stays inside the consultation, and the tool runs in any practice without an IT integration project.

Explainable by design.

An interpretable model class, per-patient reasons, calibrated probabilities and a published model card — requirements of the Medical Device Regulation and the EU AI Act, treated as product features.

Research & validation

Validated before it is used.

HETAW is being developed as research first. The symptom question set is built from published evidence and clinical review, then evaluated on retrospective data from certified endometriosis centres before any practice uses it. Results are published openly.
