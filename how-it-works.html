How it works

Three minutes at the first visit. One interpretable score.

HETAW is a decision-support app for GPs and gynaecologists. It does not diagnose and does not use insurer data: everything it scores is entered by the doctor in the consultation.

The problem

Scattered complaints, one disease, nobody connecting them

Endometriosis is tissue similar to the uterine lining growing outside the uterus — on the ovaries, the bowel, the bladder, the peritoneum, sometimes the diaphragm or nerves. Because the lesions are spread through the body, so are the symptoms. A woman sees one doctor per complaint. The first consultation is where the pattern is most often missed — and where it could most easily be caught.

10 years

Median delay from first symptoms to diagnosis in Germany and Austria — 7.7 of them after the first gynaecologist visit.

74 %

Receive at least one wrong diagnosis first — irritable bowel, cystitis, "stress" — before endometriosis is named.

510,000

Women with a confirmed diagnosis in Germany in 2024 — more than double 2005, and still below the true number affected.

Sources: Hudelist et al., Human Reproduction 2012; BARMER Arztreport 2026.

The method

A structured history in. A score with reasons out.

  1. i.
    The doctor enters a short symptom history

    Pain pattern, cycle, digestive, urinary and fatigue symptoms, reproductive history, prior consultations — designed for two to three minutes and to capture the fragments that today go unconnected.

  2. ii.
    HETAW returns a risk score with its reasons

    A calibrated pre-test risk score and the symptom patterns that drove it, in plain language: which answers raised it, which lowered it, how confident the estimate is.

  3. iii.
    The doctor decides

    Watch and re-evaluate, order ultrasound or blood work, or refer to a certified endometriosis centre. HETAW supports the decision; it never makes it, and it does not diagnose.

Three design principles

What separates HETAW from a symptom checker.

The doctor stays in charge

HETAW does not diagnose and does not communicate with the patient. It gives the doctor a structured, defensible basis for a decision he or she makes anyway.

Everything scored is entered by the doctor

No insurer, claims or electronic-record data. Consent stays inside the consultation, and the tool runs in any practice without an IT integration project.

Explainable by design

An interpretable model class, per-patient reasons, calibrated probabilities and a published model card — requirements of the MDR and the EU AI Act, treated as product features.

Research & validation

Validated before it is used

HETAW is developed as research first. The question set is built from published evidence and clinical review, then evaluated on retrospective data from certified endometriosis centres before any practice uses it. Results are published openly.

Validation partners
  1. Now

    First-visit symptom triage

    Question set development and retrospective validation with endometriosis centres.

  2. Next

    After the work-up

    Combining routine labs, ultrasound and MRI findings into a post-test likelihood.

  3. Later

    Care-pathway integration

    Pilot within a selective-care contract; CE marking as a medical device (Class IIa planned).
